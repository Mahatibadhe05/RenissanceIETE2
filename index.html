The concept

A rebirth of thought

The original Renaissance happened when art, science and philosophy stopped being treated as separate disciplines. People who painted also studied anatomy. People who studied the stars also wrote poetry. Renaissance 2026 borrows that spirit for a technical festival — a gathering where builders, thinkers and makers stop staying in their lane.

Over two days at K J Somaiya Institute of Technology, IETE KJSIT is bringing together hackers, engineers, designers and speakers for a festival built around one idea: nothing interesting gets made inside a single discipline.
